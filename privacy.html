Grove Fitness: privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Grove Fitness is a personal app used only by its developer.

What it reads

With the developer's consent, it reads the developer's own health and activity data from their Google account: activity, exercise, sleep, heart rate and body measurements.

What it does with it

The data is stored on the developer's own private server and shown back to the developer in the app. It is used for nothing else.

What it does not do

The data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any general-purpose AI model. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page, which stops all reading. Stored data is deleted by the developer on their own server.

Contact
